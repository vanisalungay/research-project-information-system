FLOW
1. Faculty Proponent
● Receives the official Call for Proposals.
● Prepares the proposal using the required format and guidelines.
● Submits the proposal to RPS for review.
2. RPS (Research and Publication Services)
● Disseminates the Call for Proposals to faculty members.
● Reviews the submitted proposal for completeness and compliance.
● If the proposal is incomplete or requires revisions, it is returned to the Faculty
Proponent for revision and resubmission.
● If the proposal is complete, it is endorsed to OVCRIGE for evaluation.
3. OVCRIGE
● Receives the endorsed proposal from RPS.
● Coordinates the evaluation process through the REC.
4. REC (Research Ethics Committee/Research Evaluation Committee)
● Conducts the technical and merit-based evaluation of the proposal.
● If the proposal is rejected or returned for revision, the decision is forwarded to
OVCRIGE.
● If the proposal passes the evaluation, the recommendation is forwarded to OVCRIGE.
OVCRIGE
● Receives the REC's evaluation result.
● Notifies the RPS of the decision through the OVCRIGE Secretariat.
● RPS notifies the Proponent about the decision.
● If the proposal is rejected or returned for revision, the Faculty Proponent revises and
resubmits it to RPS.
● If the proposal is approved, forwards it to the Office of the Chancellor for final
approval.
Office of the Chancellor
● Reviews the endorsed proposal.
● Grants the final approval for projects recommended for implementation. Grants SO
(Special Order)
● Determines whether the project requires a budget.
○ If No: The Faculty Proponent proceeds with project implementation.
○ If Yes: The proposal is forwarded to the Finance Office/OVCAF.
Finance Office / OVCAF
● Endorses and authorizes the budget for implementation.
● Releases the approved project funds.
Faculty Proponent
● Implements the approved project.
● Submits quarterly progress and financial reports.
